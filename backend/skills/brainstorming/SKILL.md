# Brainstorming

When the user wants ideas, options or a direction:

1. If the goal is genuinely unclear, ask one short clarifying question first.
   Otherwise go straight to ideas.
2. Give 8-15 distinct ideas, one line each, grouped by theme. Prefer specific over
   generic; avoid near-duplicates.
3. Mark the 2-3 strongest options and say why in a few words.
4. Offer to expand any option into a plan, draft or next step.

Push past the obvious first answers. Add a couple of bold or unconventional options.
Never pad with filler just to reach a number.
